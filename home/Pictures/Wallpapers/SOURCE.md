# Wallpapers

Converted to JPG (quality 90, at most 3840x2160) to keep the repo and ISO small.
Default: `evening-sky.jpg` (set by `apply.sh home` when DMS has no wallpaper yet).

- Catppuccin wallpapers (clear-night, clouds-night, evening-sky, fedora-black-4k,
  flatppuccin-4k-macchiato, salty-mountains, shaded-landscape, yosemite):
  [zhichaoh/catppuccin-wallpapers](https://github.com/zhichaoh/catppuccin-wallpapers), MIT License.
- The themed ones (abstract-, aerial-, calm-, cherry-, cold-, fogsmoke-, minimal-, mountain-,
  nature-, painting-): [dharmx/walls](https://github.com/dharmx/walls), collected from many
  sources; personal use.
- itachi-moon, itachi-snow: fan art of a copyrighted character
  ([Samyc2002/Anime-Wallpapers](https://github.com/Samyc2002/Anime-Wallpapers),
  [deadduck-09/FireWalls](https://github.com/deadduck-09/FireWalls)); personal use only,
  keep this repo private.
